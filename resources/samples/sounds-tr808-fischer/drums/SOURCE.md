## About

Michael Fischer's exceptionally high quality collection of hand recorded TR-808 samples, generously licensed to the commons via CC0. More information is available in the README.md file, which is from the author.

## Source and preservation

These recordings were originally published by Michael Fischer of Technopolis and redistributed as part of the DAWJ factory sample library.

- Original source: https://github.com/tidalcycles/sounds-tr808-fischer
- Retrieved: 2026-11-04
- Original repo: https://github.com/tidalcycles/sounds-tr808-fischer
- Local modifications: WAV samples renamed and moved into drums/; metadata paths updated. See RENAMING.md and sample-map.csv. Audio unchanged.

The original creators retain recognition for their work. DAWJ does not claim authorship of these recordings.
