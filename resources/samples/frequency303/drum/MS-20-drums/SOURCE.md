## About

A collection of drum samples from a Korg MS-20, the monophonic analog synthesizer introduced in 1978 and known for its aggressive sound. Distributed by Frequency 303.

## Source and preservation

These recordings were originally published by Frequency 303 and redistributed as part of the DAWJ factory sample library.
