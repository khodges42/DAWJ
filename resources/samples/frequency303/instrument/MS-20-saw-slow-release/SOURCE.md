## About

A collection of samples from the Korg MS-20, with DirectWave presets for FL Studio. This 1978 analog synth is known for its aggressive sound. Distributed by Frequency 303.

Author: Allan Legemaate
Synth: MS-20
Format: Direct Wave and wav
About: No processing was used on any of the samples
