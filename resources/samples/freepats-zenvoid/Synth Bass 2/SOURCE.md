## About
(From their website description:)

General MIDI instrument #40: Synth Bass 2. These sound samples simulate the "BASS 1" patch of the Yamaha DX7, a FM sythesis instrument that was used in many 1980s popular songs.

Recorded from Hexter software synthesizer. Hexter is a free software program that accurately models the sound generation algorithm of the original instrument. For best quality and flexibility we recommend to run Hexter synthesizer directly in your computer. These sound banks are provided for convenience when sample-based synthesizers are preferred and for building complete sound sets.
