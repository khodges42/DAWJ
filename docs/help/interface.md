# Find your way around

Deck A is on the left; Deck B is on the right. Their eight numbered parts keep
the same identities in every view. The center mixer always controls both decks.

- **Simple / Browse** gives the sound and composition browser more space.
- **Performance** presents parts, launches and the mixer.
- **Extended** adds rules, variation controls and detailed diagnostics.
- **Sketch** puts the browser and focused part's sequencer first. Switching views
  does not restart audio or change part identities.

Single-click a part to select it. The highlighted part and editor heading show
its deck, number and sound. Double-click its identity to toggle playback. Visible
play and stop controls remain available in Sketch and Performance.

Part thumbnails show the trimmed source or rhythmic pattern. The progress cursor
comes from actual engine state. Dashed borders indicate queued changes; loaded
content stays visible while stopped. Missing audio has an explicit warning.

The top timeline shows the shared bar, beat, phrase and upcoming A/B events.
The deck overviews retain local musical detail. **Overview** selects Structure,
Waveform or Hybrid, independently of the workspace or skin.

**Ctrl+K** opens searchable commands. **H** opens help outside text entry.
**Escape** dismisses ordinary dialogs; unsaved form edits require a decision.
