# Your first groove

1. DAWJ opens the configured audio output automatically, or your system default
   on first launch. It does not launch any sounds. If opening fails, use
   **Settings > Audio** to select an output and retry.
2. Choose **Sketch**, then click a part on Deck A. Empty decks can receive a new
   composition when you assign their first sound.
3. Choose a sound in the browser and press **Assign**, or drag it onto a part.
4. For a one-shot, click steps in the sequencer. The first click enables a pattern.
   Use **Play part** to hear it. Add a kick on steps 1, 5, 9 and 13, then select
   another part and add a hat on alternating steps.
5. A continuous audio loop plays as recorded. To sequence its start, choose
   **Use as step sample** first. This changes playback mode, not the audio file.
6. Repeat on Deck B. Switch to **Performance** while the music continues and use
   the center crossfader to transition between compositions.
7. Choose **File > Save As**. Save both compositions, assignments, patterns,
   regions, sections and mixer settings in one local session.

**Listen** auditions the unshaped source through the master. It is separate from
part playback; use **Play part** to hear the part's volume and low-pass filter.
